Overview

A ten-module Civil 3D programme — from interface fluency and precision drafting through survey points, TIN surface modelling, and criteria-based highway alignment design — built for infrastructure and roadway engineers across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Qatar.

AutoDesk Civil 3D — Full curriculum

Ten modules covering the complete AutoDesk Civil 3D workflow — interface and drafting foundations, points and surface modelling, and progressive alignment design from manual layout through automated, criteria-based standards compliance.

Civil 3D interface mastery

Move beyond generic AutoCAD into Prospector, Toolspace, and Panorama workflows for infrastructure modelling.

Survey-to-surface pipeline

From field survey points and description keys through TIN surfaces, contours, and earthwork volumes.

Highway geometric design

Design speed, sight distance, and horizontal/vertical curve principles that govern every alignment decision.

Criteria-based alignment

Progress from layout tools to automated standards checks aligned with regional road authority expectations.

Course curriculum — 10 modules

01
Introduction to AutoCAD Civil 3DPlatform orientation, Civil 3D vs AutoCAD, and GCC project workflow context

Establishes what makes Civil 3D different from generic AutoCAD — dynamic model-based objects, project templates, coordinate systems, and GCC delivery workflows expected by design consultancies and authorities.

02
Navigating the Civil 3D interfaceToolspace, Prospector, Panorama, and object style management

Gain fluency in Prospector, Settings, Survey, and Toolbox tabs, plus Panorama workflows and style management for standards-compliant drawing output.

03
AutoCAD modify tools for civil draftingEditing commands, grip accuracy, and right-of-way drafting standards

Apply trim, extend, offset, fillet, array, and stretch to property boundaries, easements, and ROW lines with authority-grade precision.

04
Blocks, Xrefs and annotationXref coordination, dynamic blocks, and multi-discipline drawing standards

Build Xref workflows and dynamic block libraries for civil symbology while keeping multi-discipline drawings synchronized.

05
Highway geometric design foundationsDesign speed, sight distance, and horizontal & vertical curve principles

Theory module covering AASHTO methodology as adapted by Saudi roads authorities and MOMRA, plus UAE and Qatar standards context.

06
Survey points and point cloud dataPoint groups, description keys, and field-to-finish workflows

Import coordinate files, configure description keys, organize point groups, and introduce point-cloud workflows for megaproject packages.

07
Surface modelling — TIN, grading, volumesExisting ground, design surfaces, and earthwork calculations

Build TIN surfaces, configure styles for elevation and slope analysis, and generate cut-fill quantities for cost and mobilization planning.

08
Alignment fundamentalsComponents, stationing conventions, and design criteria files

Introduce the alignment object as Civil 3D’s central roadway element — lines, curves, spirals, stationing, and label sets for plan-and-profile delivery.

09
Creating alignments with layout toolsInteractive PI-based design, curve fitting, and grip editing

Hands-on alignment creation with the Layout toolbar, fixed/floating/free curves, and realistic GCC widening and realignment scenarios.

10
Criteria-based design and complianceDesign criteria files, automated checks, and authority submission reporting

Automate standards checks for radius, superelevation, and sight distance, then generate reports for MOMRA, UAE agencies, and Qatar PWA packages.

Software, standards & platforms

AutoCAD Civil 3D Point Groups Description Keys TIN Surfaces Alignment Layout Tools Criteria-Based Design Field-to-Finish Xref Coordination MOMRA Standards AASHTO Geometry

Course outcome

On completing this course, you will be able to navigate the Civil 3D-specific workspace with fluency, build accurate survey-to-surface data pipelines, and produce fully criteria-checked horizontal alignments that comply with GCC highway design standards — skills for civil design engineer, roadway designer, and infrastructure CAD technician roles across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and Qatar.
