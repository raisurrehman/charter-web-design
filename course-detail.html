Overview

A 60–80 hour, professional-level programme that builds practical, code-compliant plumbing design competence from manual calculation through AutoCAD drafting and Revit BIM modeling — for mechanical and MEP engineers, BIM modelers and CAD technicians, engineering students, and career-starters entering plumbing design across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and the wider GCC, culminating in the Charter Center certification exam.

Plumbing Design System — Full Curriculum

Seven modules guiding learners from plumbing design foundations and code fundamentals through fixture selection, manual calculations, AutoCAD drafting, and Revit BIM modeling, followed by the Charter Center certification exam.

From calculation to AutoCAD & Revit

Fixture unit and pipe sizing first, then construction-ready AutoCAD drawings, then coordinated 3D Revit models.

Grounded in SBC 701

Design decisions anchored to the Saudi Plumbing Code, with comparative UAE and Qatar code awareness.

Real AutoCAD project

Build a complete plumbing package — layout, risers, routing, and fixture schedules — from brief to final set.

Certification exam included

Knowledge assessment plus a practical AutoCAD/Revit deliverable for Charter Center certification.

Course curriculum — 7 modules + exam

01
Plumbing Design Foundations & Core Water SystemsSystem types, water supply, hot water systems and the complete design workflow

Establishes the complete plumbing engineer’s workflow — from concept through code-compliant construction documents — covering cold water distribution, direct vs tank-fed supply, pressure zoning for high-rise GCC projects, and hot water generation and recirculation design.

02
Plumbing Codes, Standards and Engineering PrinciplesSaudi Plumbing Code (SBC 701), regional GCC standards and core engineering principles

Grounds every design decision in enforceable code requirements across water distribution, hot water, sanitary drainage, venting, and graywater rules — plus comparative awareness of Dubai, Abu Dhabi Estidama, and Qatar Construction Specifications.

03
Fixture Piping, Fittings and AccessoriesFixture units, piping materials, valves, fittings and accessory selection

Covers WSFU/DFU concepts, piping material selection for GCC conditions, valve and fitting types, and accessories that feed directly into the manual calculation modules.

04
Manual Calculations I — Water Supply SizingFixture unit demand, pipe sizing and water pressure loss calculations

Converts fixture demand into design flow, sizes pipes for velocity and pressure loss, and builds a complete pressure budget from source to fixture — including hot water generation capacity.

05
Manual Calculations II — Drainage & VentingDrainage fixture unit sizing, vent sizing and system verification

Completes the calculation package with drain diameters and slopes, vent stack design, and full-system verification before production drawings begin.

06
AutoCAD Plumbing Drafting and Project ProductionFrom layout to construction-ready drawings

The largest hands-on component: fixture placement, routing, riser diagrams, annotation against calculations, schedules, and a complete coordinated AutoCAD plumbing package.

07
Revit BIM Modeling & Advanced Systems3D modeling, advanced systems and AI-assisted design tools

Extends 2D skills into Revit BIM — families, systems, clash detection, greywater and high-rise strategies, plus AI tools as a productivity layer on top of fundamentals.

08
Charter Center Certification ExamKnowledge and practical assessment

Capstone exam with knowledge questions across modules 01–05 and a practical AutoCAD/Revit submission for Charter Center’s Plumbing Design System certification.

Software, standards & platforms

AutoCAD Plumbing Drafting Autodesk Revit BIM Saudi Plumbing Code (SBC 701) Fixture Unit & Pipe Sizing Drainage & Vent Design Water Supply & Hot Water Greywater Systems AI-Assisted Design Tools

Course outcome

On completing this course, you will be able to perform manual plumbing calculations for water supply, drainage, and venting systems, produce construction-ready AutoCAD plumbing drawings, build coordinated 3D plumbing models in Revit, and apply SBC 701 and regional GCC standards to real design scenarios — earning Charter Center certification and a practical skillset for MEP, BIM, and CAD roles across the GCC.
